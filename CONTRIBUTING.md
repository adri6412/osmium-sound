# Contributing to Osmium Sound

Thanks for considering a contribution! Pull requests and issues are welcome on
[GitHub](https://github.com/adri6412/osmium-sound).

## Workflow

- Development happens on the **`svil`** branch — target your pull requests
  there, not at `main` (which tracks production releases).
- Keep changes focused; one topic per pull request.
- User-facing strings (kiosk UI, web admin, API messages) must be provided in
  **both English and Italian** (the OS installer flow is the one exception —
  it stays English-only).
- For local development setup, see
  [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

Osmium Sound is dual-licensed (AGPL-3.0-only + commercial licenses, see
[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)). To keep that model legally possible, external
contributions require a one-time signature of the project's
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md).

Being transparent about it: the CLA lets the maintainer relicense your
contribution as part of commercial licenses of the whole project. In exchange,
the CLA commits the maintainer to keeping your contribution **always also
available under an OSI-approved open-source license** — the project cannot be
taken closed-source out from under you.

Signing is handled automatically: on your first pull request, the CLA
Assistant bot posts a comment asking you to reply with a confirmation
sentence. One click, recorded once, covers all your future contributions.
No paperwork.

## Licensing of contributions

- Contributions to the main codebase are licensed under **AGPL-3.0-only**
  (plus the CLA grant above).
- Contributions to `android-companion/` are licensed under **Apache-2.0**,
  matching its upstream ([android-squeezer](https://github.com/kaaholst/android-squeezer)).
