Osmium Sound Companion is the official remote control app for your Osmium Sound hi-fi appliance. Pair instantly by scanning the QR code shown in Osmium Sound → Settings → Phone control, then control playback from anywhere on your local network.

Features include:

&bull; Browse the library by album artist, all artists, composers, album, genre, year, playlist, favorites, or new music
&bull; Playlist control (now playing, shuffle, repeat, save/clear/modify playlists)
&bull; Manage players (names, synchronisation groups, sleep, alarms)
&bull; Full library search
&bull; Internet radio support (browse, staff picks, search)
&bull; Browse music folders
&bull; Library Views and Remote Music Libraries
&bull; Automatic discovery of Osmium Sound appliances on the local network
&bull; QR code pairing
&bull; DSP settings and system administration controls
&bull; Home Screen Widgets
&bull; Alarm Clock management
&bull; Add/remove music from favorites
&bull; Display lyrics

Osmium Sound Companion is free and open source (Apache License 2.0), built on the android-squeezer project. It only talks to your own Osmium Sound appliance over your local network — no cloud service, no tracking, no ads.
