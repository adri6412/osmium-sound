Osmium Sound Companion is the official remote control app for your Osmium Sound hi-fi appliance, and a Lyrion player in its own right. Pair instantly by scanning the QR code shown in Osmium Sound → Settings → Phone control, then control playback from anywhere on your local network.

Features include:

* Play on the phone itself: it registers with Lyrion as a player of its own, with gapless playback and a quality setting per network
* Works either with an Osmium Sound appliance (QR pairing, appliance settings) or with any other Lyrion server (host and port)
* Browse the library by album artist, all artists, composers, album, genre, year, playlist, favorites, or new music
* Playlist control (now playing, shuffle, repeat, save/clear/modify playlists)
* Manage players (names, synchronisation groups, sleep, alarms)
* Full library search
* Internet radio support (browse, staff picks, search)
* Browse music folders
* Library Views and Remote Music Libraries
* Automatic discovery of Osmium Sound appliances on the local network
* QR code pairing
* DSP settings and system administration controls
* Home Screen Widgets
* Alarm Clock management
* Add/remove music from favorites
* Display lyrics

Osmium Sound Companion is free and open source (Apache License 2.0), built on the android-squeezer project. It only talks to your own Osmium Sound appliance over your local network — no cloud service, no tracking, no ads.
